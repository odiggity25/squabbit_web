Introducing League Pro

Sep 22, 2026

We just shipped League Pro, a new subscription that unlocks a full season toolkit for the organizers who run golf leagues. Here’s what’s in it.

What League Pro unlocks

League Pro is built for the people who run leagues all season long. One subscription unlocks every league you run, with no per-league fee, and it adds the tools that make a season easier to manage and more rewarding to play in:

  • Season-long league stats: trends, records, and per-hole history for every member.
  • League sponsors: showcase sponsors on your leaderboard and printed materials all season.
  • Season schedule builder: lay out a whole season of events in one pass.
  • Draft events: build events privately, then publish them when they are ready.
  • Bulk event actions: publish, complete, or cancel many events at once.
  • Scheduled announcements: announce new events to members on your own schedule.
  • Multiple admins: share the work of running the league across a team.

League Pro is $249.99 a year. See everything it includes on the League Pro page, or compare it with our other plans on the pricing page.

Plan a whole season in minutes

Setting up a league used to mean adding events one at a time. The new season schedule builder lets you pick a template event, choose how often you play (every week, every two weeks, monthly, or every few days), and generate the whole season at once. If you play nine holes, it can even alternate front and back nine down the schedule.

Manage a season without the busywork

Once your season is laid out, League Pro keeps it easy to run. Build events as private drafts and publish them when you are ready, or schedule each draft to go live automatically a set number of days before its own date. Need to change several events at once? Select them and publish, complete, or cancel the whole batch in a single step.

Update to the latest version of Squabbit to get everything above.

Ready to run a standout season? Explore League Pro or see all plans.
