AI league season recaps and season awards

Sep 28, 2026

A golf league season is a long story. Weekly nights stack up from spring to fall, standings swing, someone quietly gets a lot better, and someone else keeps finding new ways to lose on the last hole. By the time the final round is in, most of that has already blurred together. It deserves a proper send-off.

League Pro can now write it for you. Squabbit reads back through your whole season, every event, the standings, the results, and turns it into an AI-generated recap of how the year actually played out. Then it caps it off with a set of end-of-season awards, the kind you would hand out at the banquet if anyone had kept track all year. Now something has.

A recap of the whole season

This is not a table of final standings. Squabbit looks at the arc of the season and tells the story: who set the pace early, who made a late charge, the weeks that decided the title, and the rivalries that ran all year. It pulls out the moments that mattered and threads them together so the recap reads like a season wrap-up, not a spreadsheet.

It works no matter how your league is set up. Whether you run a season-long points race, a handicap net league, or a mix of formats week to week, the recap is built from your league's real results, so it reflects the season your group actually played.

End-of-season awards

The fun part. Along with the recap, Squabbit hands out season awards drawn from the year's data. The season champion is the obvious one, but the rest is where a league night comes alive: most improved, most consistent, the biggest single-round score, the heartbreaker who kept coming up just short. Some awards celebrate, some gently roast, all of them come straight from the numbers so nobody can argue they were rigged.

Who it is for

If you run a league, this is the easiest end-of-season summary you will ever put together. Instead of digging through weeks of scorecards to write a wrap-up email or figure out who earned what, you generate the recap and awards in a tap. It is the perfect thing to read out at the final night, drop in the group chat, or pin so the whole league can look back on it.

And it is not just for the commissioner. Once generated, the recap and awards are saved on the league, so every member opens the app and finds the season laid out for them, awards and all. It gives everyone something to celebrate, something to argue about, and a reason to come back for next season.

The AI season recap and season awards are part of League Pro. Update to the latest version of Squabbit to try them.
