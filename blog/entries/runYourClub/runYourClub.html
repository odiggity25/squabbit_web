Run your whole golf club in Squabbit

Oct 11, 2026

Most golf clubs run on a patchwork: a spreadsheet for the roster, a separate tool for dues, email threads for events, and a group chat for everything else. Squabbit pulls all of it into one place. You can run your entire club here: members, dues, events, and communication, all in the same app your golfers already use to keep score.

Everything below splits into two parts. The membership side of a club is completely free for every club. Club Pro is the optional layer on top that adds the premium toolkit for larger, busier clubs.

Memberships, free for every club

Set up your club and invite your golfers, and you have a living roster instead of a stale spreadsheet. On top of that, you can run real memberships, and this costs nothing.

Create membership tiers for how your club is actually organized: a Full tier with full dues, a Social tier at a lower price, a Junior tier at a reduced rate, and so on. Each tier has its own name, benefits, and dues that recur automatically every year or every month. Keep it as simple as a single free tier, or set real prices and let members pay.

When it comes to collecting dues, you pick how per tier. Charge members automatically through your club's connected Stripe account, where the yearly or monthly renewal bills itself, or collect offline (cash, cheque, Venmo, bank transfer) and mark each member paid when the money comes in. Members handle their own dues from the club's Membership tab: they see what they owe and tap Pay dues to check out by card, or to let the club know they have paid in person.

All of it, creating tiers, setting recurring dues, collecting them through Stripe or offline, and managing your roster, is free. There is no subscription behind memberships and no per-member charge. For the full walkthrough, see Club Memberships: tiers and dues.

Club Pro: the premium club toolkit

Memberships get you up and running. Club Pro is for clubs that want more control and a more polished, branded experience. It is one account-wide subscription at $399.99 a year, and it includes League, Tournament, and Player Pro too, so every event you run under the club gets the full treatment.

Here is what Club Pro adds:

  • Member groups. Sort your roster into named groups (Men's League, Ladies League, Juniors, Seniors, Staff), and a member can belong to several at once.
  • Event visibility. Choose who sees each event: everyone in the club, or just the groups you pick. The right members see the right events, and no one's calendar gets cluttered.
  • Club branding. Swap generic green for your own accent color and logo across your club, and every tournament and league you run inside it inherits that look.
  • Multiple club admins. Share the work with your committee instead of running everything from one account.
  • Club sponsors. Feature your sponsors across the club and track their impressions and clicks.
  • Richer club chat. Post videos and high-resolution photos in your club's chat, not just compressed snapshots.
  • Organizer tools. Draft events, bulk event actions, scheduled announcements, and an admin activity log that shows exactly what changed and who did it.

Member groups and event visibility are designed to work hand in hand: build your groups once, then point each event at the groups that should see it. The ladies league night goes to the ladies league, the junior clinic goes to the juniors, and the club championship stays visible to everyone.

For more on these, see Member groups and event visibility and Club branding: your color and logo.

Create a club and set up memberships for free, then unlock the premium toolkit with Club Pro whenever you're ready.
