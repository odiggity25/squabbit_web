Data Processing Agreement

Squabbit — Free Golf Tournament & League Software

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the agreement between Squabbit ("Processor"), operator of the Squabbit golf event platform, and the Customer ("Controller"), the organisation or individual who creates and administers events, leagues, or tournaments using Squabbit.

It governs the processing of personal data that the Processor carries out on the Controller's behalf, and is provided to support compliance with Article 28 of the UK General Data Protection Regulation ("UK GDPR") and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Definitions

Terms such as "personal data", "processing", "data subject", "controller", "processor", and "supervisory authority" have the meanings given to them in the UK GDPR. "Applicable Data Protection Law" means the UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018, and any other data protection or privacy laws that apply to the processing. "Sub-processor" means any third party engaged by the Processor to process personal data on the Controller's behalf.

2. Roles of the Parties

The Controller is the controller of the personal data it uploads to or generates within Squabbit. The Processor processes that personal data only on behalf of, and on the documented instructions of, the Controller, as set out in this DPA and the Squabbit Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

3. Subject Matter, Nature, and Purpose of Processing

The Processor processes personal data solely to provide the Squabbit platform: creating and running golf events, leagues, and tournaments; scoring; handicapping; leaderboards; participant communication; fundraising features; and related functionality requested by the Controller.

  • Duration: For as long as the Controller maintains an active account or event, and thereafter only for the limited retention period described in Section 8.
  • Categories of data subjects: Event organisers, participants/players, and other individuals the Controller adds to an event (for example team members and guests).
  • Types of personal data: Names, email addresses, profile details, golf scores and handicaps, event participation records, and (where applicable) payment-related metadata. Squabbit does not require special-category data and asks that Controllers do not upload it.

4. Processor Obligations

The Processor shall:

  • Process personal data only on the Controller's documented instructions, including this DPA, unless required to do otherwise by law (in which case it will inform the Controller unless legally prohibited).
  • Ensure that persons authorised to process the personal data are bound by an appropriate duty of confidentiality.
  • Implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures (see Section 6).
  • Respect the conditions in Sections 5 and 7 for engaging sub-processors and transferring data internationally.
  • Taking into account the nature of the processing, assist the Controller by appropriate measures in responding to requests from data subjects exercising their rights (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection).
  • Assist the Controller in ensuring compliance with its obligations regarding security, breach notification, data protection impact assessments, and prior consultation.
  • At the Controller's choice, delete or return all personal data at the end of the provision of services, except where retention is required by law (see Section 8).
  • Make available to the Controller information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with Article 28, and allow for and contribute to audits as described in Section 9.

The Processor will impose data-protection terms on each sub-processor no less protective than those in this DPA, and will give the Controller reasonable notice of any intended addition or replacement of a sub-processor so that the Controller may object on reasonable data-protection grounds.

6. Security Measures

The Processor maintains technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including:

  • Data stored on Google Cloud infrastructure, encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.
  • Access controls and authentication restricting access to personal data to authorised personnel and to the event's own participants/administrators, enforced by server-side security rules.
  • Segregation of deleted data into restricted collections pending permanent erasure.
  • Ongoing review of security practices in line with the nature of the processing.

7. International Data Transfers

Personal data is stored and processed in the United States by the sub-processors listed in Section 5. Where personal data of UK data subjects is transferred to the United States, that transfer is made under an appropriate safeguard recognised by Applicable Data Protection Law, which may include the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (where the sub-processor is certified), the UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA), or the International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Google Cloud provides such safeguards for the infrastructure services above.

8. Retention and Deletion

  • Personal data is retained for as long as the Controller maintains an active account or event.
  • When the Controller (or a participant) deletes a tournament, league, round, or user account, the affected data is moved to a restricted, deleted state and is automatically and permanently purged after 30 days.
  • On termination of the services, the Processor will, at the Controller's choice, delete the personal data, subject to the same 30-day automated purge and to any retention required by law.

9. Audit

The Processor will make available to the Controller information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA and Article 28 UK GDPR, and will respond to reasonable written audit enquiries. Any on-site audit will be limited to once per year (absent a specific regulatory or breach-driven reason), on reasonable notice, during business hours, and subject to confidentiality.

10. Personal Data Breach

The Processor will notify the Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting the Controller's personal data, and will provide the Controller with information reasonably available to assist the Controller in meeting its own breach-notification obligations.

11. No Sale or Independent Use

The Processor does not sell personal data and does not use the Controller's participant data for its own marketing or for any third-party marketing purpose. The Processor uses the data only to provide and maintain the service.

12. General

This DPA is governed by the laws of England and Wales. In the event of a conflict between this DPA and the Squabbit Terms of Service on matters of data protection, this DPA prevails. If any provision is found unenforceable, the remainder stays in effect.
