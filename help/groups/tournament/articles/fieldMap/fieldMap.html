The Field Map

The Field map is a live map of your course that shows where every group is while your tournament is being played. As an admin you can glance at it and see the whole field at once – who is where, and how the day is moving – without walking the course or calling the starter. It was previously called the course map, and is now the Field map.

What it shows

The map plots each group out on the course, so you can see the field spread across the holes in one view. Tap a group to see more about it, such as which hole it is on and how its round is progressing. It is the quickest way to answer “where is everyone?” during an event.

It updates in real time

You do not have to refresh anything. As players move around the course and enter their scores, the map keeps itself up to date, so what you are looking at reflects where the field actually is right now.

Players share their location, with their consent

Players appear on the map because they have chosen to share their location with you. When a player opens their scorecard in a tournament that has the Field map turned on, Squabbit asks them once whether they want to share their location with the organizer. If they agree, their group shows up on your map; if they decline, they simply are not plotted. Nobody is tracked without opting in.

Good to know: Location sharing is tied to the scorecard being open during the round, so it only happens while a player is actively using Squabbit to play. A player who declined can change their mind, and one who opted in is only shared for that event.

It is for admins

The Field map is an admin tool. Players see the normal scoring experience; only admins get the top-down view of the whole field. That keeps it a behind-the-scenes aid for whoever is running the event, not something the field is watching.

Where to find it

Open your tournament and go to the Schedule tab. For admins there is a Field map option alongside the other schedule views – tap it to open the live map. The option only appears for admins.

Unlocking the Field map

The Field map is part of Tournament Pro. Tournament Pro is available as a yearly subscription or as a one-time unlock for a single event, and it brings the Field map along with the rest of the admin toolkit.
