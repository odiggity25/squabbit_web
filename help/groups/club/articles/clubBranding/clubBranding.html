Club branding: your color and logo

By default, everything in Squabbit uses our signature green. Branding lets you swap that out for your own look: pick an accent color and upload a logo, and your club’s pages take on your identity instead of a generic one. Best of all, that look carries through to every tournament and league you run inside the club, so your members see your club everywhere.

What branding changes

Branding has two parts, and you can set either one on its own:

  • Accent color. The color used for buttons, highlights, and the header across your pages and events. Every color option is chosen to keep header text readable, so you can’t pick something that makes your pages hard to read.
  • Logo. A small square mark shown in your club’s header. Use your club crest, course logo, or any image you like.

Where to set it

Branding lives in your club’s settings. To get there:

  • Open your club and go to Club setup›Display.
  • Find the Branding section.
  • Tap Accent color to open the color picker and choose your color.
  • Tap Logo to pick an image from your device and upload it.

Your choices save and apply right away. To change a logo later, just tap the Logo row again and pick a new one. To remove it, tap the small clear (×) button on the Logo row and your header goes back to showing no logo.

Tip: A clean, square logo on a transparent or simple background looks best in the header, since it’s rendered as a small rounded badge.

Where your branding shows up

Once set, your accent color and logo appear throughout the app wherever your club is shown:

  • The club header at the top of your club’s page, where your logo sits and your accent color sets the tone.
  • The sub-pages and screens inside your club, where buttons and highlights pick up your accent color.
  • The tournaments and leagues you run inside the club, which inherit the club’s branding automatically, so an event under your club looks like your club rather than generic Squabbit green.

You don’t have to set branding on each event one by one. Because tournaments and leagues under your club inherit its branding, their own Display settings show a read-only row (“Uses your club’s branding”) pointing you back to the club to change the color and logo in one place.

Branding across clubs, leagues, and tournaments

Branding isn’t only for clubs. The same accent color and logo settings are available on standalone leagues and tournaments too, so you can give any of your groups its own identity. A club is just the easiest way to brand many events at once: set it on the club, and everything inside it follows along.

Unlocking branding

Custom branding is a Club Pro feature. Once Club Pro is active, the Accent color and Logo rows become editable and your branding applies everywhere your club appears. (On leagues and tournaments, branding is part of League Pro and Host Pro respectively.)

If you tap Accent color or Logo before Club Pro is active, Squabbit shows you what Club Pro includes so you can unlock it and start branding.
