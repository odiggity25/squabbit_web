Elimination Golf Format

Also known as: Knockout, Single-Elimination Bracket, Match Play Bracket

Elimination is golf’s bracket format — a single-elimination knockout where players (or teams) face off in 1 vs. 1 matches and one loss sends you home. The winner of each match advances to the next round, and the field is cut in half round after round until a single champion is left standing. It’s the format behind classic match play championships, and Squabbit builds and advances the whole bracket for you.

The Rules
  1. Every player (or team) is seeded into a single-elimination bracket.
  2. In each round, opponents are paired and play a 1 vs. 1 match against each other.
  3. The winner of each match advances to the next round; the loser is knocked out.
  4. This repeats round after round — the field is cut in half each time — until only one player or team remains as the champion.
Byes: When the number of entrants isn’t an exact power of two (such as 2, 4, 8, 16, or 32), some players get a first-round bye and advance automatically. Squabbit handles the bracket seeding and byes for you.

Example

Eight players are seeded into a bracket and play down to a single winner:

8-Player Bracket
1. Quarterfinals: Four matches are played — A vs. H, D vs. E, C vs. F, and B vs. G. A, D, C, and B win their matches and advance.
2. Semifinals: The four winners are paired — A vs. D and C vs. B. A and B win and move on to the final.
3. Final: A faces B head to head. A closes out the match 3 & 2.
Champion: Player A — the last one standing

Team Elimination

Team Elimination is the same knockout bracket, but teams fill the bracket instead of individuals. Two teams meet in each match, the winning team advances, and the losing team is eliminated — round after round until one team is left standing.

The difference is how each bracket match is played. In the individual version, every match is a Match Play contest decided hole by hole. In Team Elimination, each match is played as a chosen team game — for example Best Ball, Scramble, or Alternate Shot — and the team that wins that game advances.

Note: Choose Team Elimination when teams play the bracket. The individual version is simply called Elimination.

Elimination vs Match Play

Both formats are head to head, so it’s easy to mix them up — but they decide a winner very differently.

Match Play is a hole-by-hole contest where two sides compete and the side that wins the most holes takes the match. Elimination is a knockout bracket built on top of those matches: there’s no second chance — a single loss sends you home, and the field keeps halving until one player or team remains.

In short: Match Play is how a single head-to-head match is scored, while Elimination is the bracket that strings a series of those matches together into a tournament.

Setting Up in Squabbit

To run an elimination bracket in Squabbit:

  1. Create a new tournament.
  2. Under format, choose Elimination (for individuals) or Team Elimination (for teams).
  3. Add your players, or set up your teams and add players to each.
  4. For Team Elimination, pick the team game used for the matches — such as Best Ball, Scramble, or Alternate Shot.
  5. Squabbit builds the bracket, handles seeding and byes, and advances winners automatically as matches are completed.
Note: Individual Elimination matches are played as Match Play, decided hole by hole. The last player or team to win their way through every round is the champion.
