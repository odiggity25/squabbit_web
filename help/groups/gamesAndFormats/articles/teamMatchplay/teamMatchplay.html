Game Format

Team Matchplay Golf Format

Also known as: Matchplay (team version)

Team Matchplay is the team version of match play. Each round of the tournament, your teams — called sides — play against each other, and the result of each match earns points for the team. You pick the game format used for each round, from Best Ball to Scramble to Alternate Shot, and you set the point values for a win, loss, and tie. When the dust settles, the side with the most match points takes the trophy. It’s flexible, team-friendly, and perfect for everything from a weekend rivalry to a multi-day cup.

The Rules
  1. Divide everyone into teams, which are called sides. You can add as many teams as you like.
  2. In each round, the teams play matches against each other using a game format you choose for that round.
  3. The outcome of each match earns points for the team — based on whether it was a win, loss, or tie.
  4. You add up each team’s match points across all rounds.
  5. The team with the most match points wins.
Multi-round flexibility: You can run Team Matchplay over as many tournament rounds as you like, and choose a different game format for each round — Best Ball one round, Alternate Shot the next, and so on.

Example

Two teams — Team A and Team B — play a round as Best Ball, with a win worth 1 point and a tie worth ½ point:

Round 1 — Best Ball
1. Both sides play the match as Best Ball, each player counting their own ball and the low score per team deciding each hole.
2. Team A wins the match → 1 pt Team A.
3. Team B loses the match → 0 pts Team B.
Points tally: Team A 1 – Team B 0

Add another round in a different format and the points keep stacking up. Once every round is played, the team with the highest total wins.

Choosing the Match Format

Each tournament round in a Team Matchplay event uses a game format that you choose — and you can use a different one in every round. Common choices include Best Ball (Fourball), Scramble, and Alternate Shot, among many others. This lets you keep the event varied: a Scramble round to loosen everyone up, an Alternate Shot round for the pressure, a Best Ball round to finish.

The points awarded for each match result are fully configurable per round. You decide how many points a win, a loss, and a tie are worth, so you can weight certain rounds more heavily or keep every round equal — whatever suits your event.

Team Matchplay vs Ryder Cup

Team Matchplay and the Ryder Cup format have a lot in common: both are team formats, both can run over multiple rounds, and both decide the winner on points.

The key difference is the team structure. A Ryder Cup splits everyone into exactly two sides who face off across a series of matches each round. Team Matchplay is more open-ended — you can add any number of teams, not just two, which makes it a natural fit for a multi-team round robin or a larger group.

Looking for the individual version, where single players go head-to-head? See Match Play.

Setting Up in Squabbit

To create a Team Matchplay tournament in Squabbit:

  1. Create a new tournament.
  2. Choose Team Matchplay as the format.
  3. Add your teams — as many as you want.
  4. For each tournament round, choose the game format and set the points for a win, loss, and tie.
  5. Assign players to their teams.
Note: As matches are played, enter scores in the app and Squabbit works out each match result and tallies the team point totals automatically.
