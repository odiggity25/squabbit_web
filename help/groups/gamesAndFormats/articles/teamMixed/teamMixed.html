Game Format

Mixed Golf Format

Also known as: Mixed Team Format

Mixed is a team format built for multi-round events where you want variety instead of the same game every round. You choose a different team game for each round — for example Best Ball on day one, a Scramble on day two, and Alternate Shot to finish — and Squabbit combines all the rounds into one overall team standing. It’s a great way to keep a multi-day outing or weekend trip feeling fresh.

The Rules
  1. Set up a multi-round team tournament — one round per day, or several rounds in a single event.
  2. Choose a different team game for each round (for example Best Ball, then Scramble, then Alternate Shot).
  3. Each round is played and scored by that game’s own rules.
  4. The results of every round combine into one overall standing, and the team with the best combined result wins.
Note: This is a team format built for multi-round or multi-day events where you want variety instead of the same game every round.

Example

A team is playing a three-round Mixed event:

Three-Round Mixed Event
1. Round 1 — Best Ball. Everyone plays their own ball and the team takes the best score on each hole.
2. Round 2 — Scramble. Everyone hits every shot and the team plays on from the best one, posting a single team score per hole.
3. Round 3 — Alternate Shot. Partners share one ball and take turns hitting it, which rewards steady, mistake-free golf.
Overall: combined team result across all three rounds

Which Games Can You Mix

You can assign any of Squabbit’s team games to a round, and you can mix and match them however you like — there’s no requirement that the rounds share a theme. Some of the most popular choices are:

  • Best Ball
  • 1-2-3 Best Ball
  • Scramble
  • Shamble
  • Chapman
  • Alternate Shot
  • Ambrose
  • Low Ball Low Total
  • Team Quota
  • Worst Ball

Other team games such as Best Balls, Cha Cha Cha, Liv, Lone Ranger, One/Two Gross/Net, and Team Average are available too, so you can tailor each round to your group.

Setting Up in Squabbit

To create a Mixed game in Squabbit:

  1. Create a new tournament with more than one round (one per day, or several rounds in the same event).
  2. Under format, choose Mixed.
  3. Pick the team game you want to play for each round.
  4. Add teams and assign players to them.

Each round is then scored by the rules of the game you chose for it, and Squabbit rolls the rounds together into one overall team standing.

Tip: Mixed shines on multi-day trips — give each day its own game so the event never feels repetitive while still crowning one overall winning team.
