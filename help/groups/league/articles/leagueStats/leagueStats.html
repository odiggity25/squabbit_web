League Stats

League Stats is a League Pro feature that tracks season-long statistics across your whole league. Instead of looking at one event at a time, you get the bigger picture: scoring trends, records, and per-hole history, all kept automatically for every member as your events are played.

There’s nothing to enter by hand. As players post scores in each event, Squabbit rolls those results up into league stats behind the scenes, so the numbers are always up to date.

Where to find it

From your league, open the Leaderboard tab and tap the Stats chip at the top.

League Stats is unlocked once for the whole league by an admin. After it’s unlocked, every member of the league can open the Stats chip and browse the season stats, not just admins. There’s no per-player setup, and members don’t need their own subscription to view them.

What you get

The Stats view is organized into categories you tab through at the top, and it collects dozens of season-long numbers for your league. The categories include:

  • Combined and round scores: best, worst, and average gross and net scores for every player across the season.
  • Hole scores and hole stats: how the league and each player perform hole by hole over the year, down to individual holes.
  • Streaks: hot and cold runs, like consecutive pars or birdies, tracked across the season.
  • Competitions: season results from on-course contests such as closest to the pin and longest drive.
  • Placements: how often each player finishes in each position across your events.
  • Per-player breakdowns: tap any member to see all of their own numbers in one place.

Each category is sortable, so you can rank the whole league by any stat, and there are more categories than will fit on screen at once, so scroll the chips to see them all.

Because these stats span the whole season rather than a single event, they’re a great way to spot who’s heating up, which holes play the hardest for your group, and how the field is trending as the year goes on.

Tip: League Stats get richer the more you play. Early in the season there won’t be much to show, but as more events are completed the trends, records, and per-hole history fill in and become more useful.

Unlocking League Stats

League Stats is part of League Pro. A single League Pro subscription unlocks it, along with all the other league tools, for every league you run. You don’t need a separate purchase per league: one subscription covers them all.

Once an admin has League Pro and unlocks it for a league, the Stats chip is available to everyone in that league.

Note: If you don’t see any numbers yet after unlocking, it usually just means not enough events have been completed. Play a few more events and the stats will start to populate.
