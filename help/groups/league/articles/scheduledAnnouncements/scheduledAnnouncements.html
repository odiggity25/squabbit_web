Scheduled Announcements

Scheduled Announcements is a League Pro feature that controls when your members are notified about an event. You can publish an event now but have Squabbit announce it to your members later, on a schedule you choose, instead of notifying everyone the moment it goes live.

Why use it

Publishing an event and announcing it to your members don’t have to happen at the same time. You might set up and publish several events well ahead of the season, but you don’t want members getting a stack of notifications all at once. Scheduling the announcements spaces them out, so each event is announced closer to when it’s actually played.

It also keeps your league feeling active without any weekly effort from you: line the announcements up once, and members hear about each event right on cue.

How it works

When you publish an event, choose Schedule the announcement instead of notifying members straight away. The event goes live now, but the announcement to your members is held until the time you pick.

For a run of events, you can schedule announcements in bulk from the Events tab. Each announcement is then sent a set number of days before its own event date, so a whole season of events can each announce themselves at, say, three days out, automatically. A scheduled announcement simply waits until its time arrives, and you can cancel a pending one beforehand if plans change.

Scheduled announcements vs. scheduled go-live: These are two different tools. Scheduling go-live keeps an event hidden as a draft until it publishes itself. A scheduled announcement is for an event that is already published, and only controls when your members are notified about it.

Unlocking Scheduled Announcements

Scheduled Announcements is part of League Pro. A single subscription unlocks it for every league you run, so there’s no need to buy it separately for each one.

Announcements on the group’s home tab

Separate from event announcements, every tournament, league, and club has an Announcements section on its home tab. An admin can post a short, pinned note there (a reminder, a weather call, a tee-time change) and it shows at the top of the group’s home tab until it expires, then stays in the chat like any other message. Posting one right away is free for admins.

Posting an announcement

  • Open the group and go to the home tab.
  • In the Announcements section, tap Post announcement.
  • Type your message, then choose how long it should stay pinned under Show on home for (1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks, until you remove it, or a custom end date).
  • Leave When to post on Post now and tap Post announcement.

To take a pinned announcement down early, tap the × next to it on the home tab. That removes it from the home page; the message itself stays in the chat.

Scheduling an announcement for later

You don’t have to post an announcement the moment you write it. In the Post announcement sheet, switch When to post from Post now to Schedule later, then pick the date and time it should go live. Squabbit holds the announcement and posts it from you at that time, up to 30 days out. The “Show on home for” window then counts from the go-live time, not from when you wrote it.

Scheduling needs the group’s Pro tier. Posting an announcement now is free, but scheduling one to go out later is part of the group’s own Pro subscription (Host Pro for tournaments, League Pro for leagues, or Club Pro for clubs). If the group isn’t on Pro yet, tapping Schedule later shows the upgrade screen first.

Editing or cancelling a scheduled announcement

While an announcement is still waiting to go live, the Announcements section on the home tab shows a Scheduled row with a count of how many are pending (admins only). Tap it to open the list.

  • Edit: tap a scheduled announcement to reopen it, change the wording, go-live time, or how long it stays pinned, then save. It keeps its new time and goes out then.
  • Cancel: tap Cancel next to it and confirm. It won’t be posted, and you can schedule a new one any time.

Once a scheduled announcement goes live it moves into the pinned list and the chat like a normal post, so edit or cancel it before its go-live time if plans change.

Scheduling a regular message (Player Pro)

Announcements are an admin tool, but anyone can line up an ordinary chat message to send later. In any chat, open the message options and choose Schedule message: write your message, pick a future time (up to 30 days out), and Squabbit sends it to that chat from you at that time. You’ll see your own pending scheduled messages listed there too, and you can cancel one before it sends.

Schedule message is a Player Pro feature. Unlike scheduling an admin announcement (which uses the group’s Pro tier), scheduling your own chat message is unlocked by your personal Player Pro subscription and works in any chat you’re in.
